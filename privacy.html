Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What this prototype stores

Better Hacker has no user accounts and does not create a server-side learner profile. Lesson completion, the four guided-exercise count, seven investigation completion states, and the most recent completed Course Review result are stored in your browser’s localStorage on this device.

Daily Challenge, XP, and streaks

The versioned Daily Challenge state stores a bounded history of completed local dates, challenge IDs, awarded challenge XP, and current/longest streak values. XP, levels, achievements, and skill badges are calculated locally from validated learning and challenge evidence. None of this information is public or synchronized.

Course Review results

The stored Course Review result includes your score, percentage, completion time, and per-topic summary. It remains in your browser and is used to restore the dashboard, result summary, recommendation, and Knowledge Checkpoint achievement.

Waitlist email

If you submit the waitlist form, your normalized email address is sent to Formspree at the endpoint configured in this site. Better Hacker stores the email in localStorage only after Formspree confirms a successful request. Formspree processes the submitted email under its own privacy practices.

Learning Assistant

The current Learning Assistant and Byte Learning Guide use authored, rule-based guidance. It does not send questions to a live AI model and does not perform personalized AI analysis.

Educational badges

Better Hacker skill badges and achievements are local educational indicators. They are not accredited certifications or professional qualifications.

Your controls

Reset Learning Progress removes Better Hacker lesson, exercise, investigation, Course Review, Daily Challenge, XP-award, and streak state; derived achievements and badges reset with that evidence from this browser while preserving a confirmed waitlist email. You can also clear site data using your browser controls.

Prototype limitations

Because learning state is local, it is not synchronized between devices and may be lost if browser storage is cleared. Do not store sensitive information in lesson or review answer fields.
